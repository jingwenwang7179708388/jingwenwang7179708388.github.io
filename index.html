Sponsor-aware job finder

Personal tool · 2026

International new grads waste hours on jobs that will never sponsor a visa. I joined 4.27M H-1B filings with live postings from 7 job boards, then ranked matches with hybrid search, LLM scoring and a learned reranker. I use it in my own job search.

It also runs as an MCP server, so an assistant like Claude can search and explain matches for me.

Seeing encampments

USC research

At USC's Integrated Media Systems Center I fine-tuned a detector on Street View images I annotated myself (0.82 mAP@0.5), then placed each detection on the map and merged duplicates seen from different angles.

The same tent shows up in many photos. Matching them by location and visual similarity lifted precision from 0.77 to 0.83.
